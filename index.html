Maharashtra on a plate, region by region.

Ten guides to the dishes, flavors and food traditions of Nagpur, Jalgaon, Kolhapur, Pune, Konkan and beyond.

Maharashtra's Regional Cuisines: A Food Map from Konkan to Vidarbha

Explore Maharashtra's food regions: Konkan, Desh, Khandesh, Marathwada, Kolhapur and Vidarbha, and the signature dishes that define each one.

Saoji Food of Nagpur: The Fiery Cuisine Born in Vidarbha

Discover Nagpur's Saoji cuisine: its spicy mutton and chicken gravies, the signature Saoji masala, and where this bold Vidarbha tradition comes from.

Jalgaon and Khandesh Food: Varan Batti, Bharit and Black Masala

A tour of Khandesh cooking around Jalgaon: varan batti, smoky vangyache bharit, shev bhaji, thecha and the bold kala masala behind them.

Kolhapuri Food: Tambda Pandhra Rassa, Misal and Fiery Thecha

Kolhapur is famous for its bold cooking. Learn about tambda and pandhra rassa, Kolhapuri misal, mutton thali and the spice behind them.

Pune Food Guide: Misal Pav, Bakarwadi, Shrikhand and More

From Puneri misal pav to crisp bakarwadi and creamy shrikhand, explore the snacks, sweets and old-city eateries that define Pune's food culture.

Konkan and Malvan Food: Fish Curry, Sol Kadhi and Kombdi Vade

Discover coastal Maharashtra's cooking: Malvani fish curry, kombdi vade, sol kadhi, and the coconut and kokum flavors of the Konkan coast.

Mumbai Street Food: Vada Pav, Pav Bhaji and Beyond

A guide to Mumbai's street food classics: vada pav, pav bhaji, misal, bhel and kothimbir vadi, and what makes them part of daily city life.

Marathwada Food: Jowar Bhakri, Zunka, Pithla and Thecha

Marathwada's cuisine is rustic and filling: jowar bhakri, zunka, pithla, thecha and Aurangabad's famous naan qalia are the dishes to know.

Varhadi Food of Vidarbha: Patodi Rassa, Kadhi and Wedding Feasts

Varhadi cuisine from Vidarbha features patodi rassa, kadhi, spicy mutton and hearty thalis. Learn what makes this region's food so distinctive.

Maharashtrian Sweets and Festival Food: Puran Poli, Modak and Shrikhand

From puran poli at Gudi Padwa to modak at Ganesh Chaturthi, discover the sweets and festival dishes that mark the Maharashtrian calendar.
